## GraphModule

The activity graph adapts the bounded-resolution/downsampling approach from
[boatbeaker/GraphModule](https://github.com/boatbeaker/GraphModule), then uses
Roblox's native `Path2D` UI primitive instead of its original per-segment Frame
renderer.
